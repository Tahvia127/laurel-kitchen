Who we are

A kitchen that acts like a front porch

Laurel started in 2021 with one oven, four tables and a rule we still keep: nobody gets rushed. We fire pizza at 800 degrees, roll pasta every morning, and buy from growers we can drive to.

The room is loud, the lights are warm and the playlist is somebody's actual mixtape. Come in a sweatshirt. Bring the baby. Stay for one more.

Before you ask

Questions we get every week

Still stuck? Call us. A person picks up.

(555) 555-0148
Do you take reservations?

Yes, for parties of one to six, up to 30 days out. The bar and the patio are kept for walk-ins every night.

How late does the kitchen run?

The kitchen stops 30 minutes before close. The bar keeps going until the lights come up.

Can you handle allergies?

Most dishes can be made gluten free or dairy free, and the kitchen keeps a separate prep area. Tell your server, or add a note to your online order. Our fryer is shared, so it is not safe for severe allergies.

Is there a kids menu?

There is, and kids eat free on Sundays with any adult entree.

Do you deliver?

We do, about two miles in every direction, with our own drivers. $20 minimum and a flat $3.99.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, in any amount from $25. They never expire and work online or at the table.

Can I bring a big group?

Seven or more becomes a private event so nobody waits an hour for food. The back room seats 24.

Are you hiring?

Usually. Email jobs@laurelkitchen.example with what you want to do and when you can start.
