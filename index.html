Neighborhood Kitchen · Chicago

Laurel is a neighborhood kitchen in Logan Square, built on the idea that a good dinner does not need to be complicated. We cook seasonally, we bake our own bread, and we keep the wine list short and honest. Come as you are, stay as long as you like.
